# Resolve material contradictions

Keep Roger's voice and account of his day. Verify independently checkable status and deployment claims against evidence. His latest correction can supersede an older record; note which record is stale instead of treating it as a new blocker.

A contradiction is material when it changes what happened, why work slipped, what unblocks it, whether a feature is available, or what someone is being asked to do. Cosmetic naming differences and a clearly older status do not need another interview.

Before final copy:

1. State the two conflicting facts in one sentence, with their sources.
2. Ask one focused question that would settle the explanation. Example: "The archive-tier change already merged; the open subscription change only stores plans. Is that open change actually blocking the import, or are you choosing to wait for its review?"
3. Save the question under `## Clarifications` in today's notes with status `open`. Pause final drafting and rendering until Roger answers. Continue unrelated gathering only if useful.
4. Record his answer, its scope and status `answered`. Use it in both updates; don't re-ask in internal mode.

An unresolved contradiction cannot be hidden by replacing "depends on" with "while I wait for" and moving the problem to private notes. That still leaves the reader with the same causal explanation.

If Roger explicitly wants a draft without answering, remove the disputed claim or causal link and label the remaining uncertainty plainly. Record `accepted uncertainty` in the clarification. Do not call the result factually settled. Automated replay mode may supply its own no-questions policy; keep that limitation in the replay result.

Ownership is not an unresolved ask. When Roger or the evidence confirms someone is already handling a discussion, record the owner in today's notes. Mention its status only if it helps the readers; generate a new ask only for a new decision or intervention Roger needs. Ownership does not prove completion or a particular decision.
